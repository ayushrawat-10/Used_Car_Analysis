Data Cleaning Decisions

  • 1 duplicate row removed to avoid model bias.
  • 3 zero-price listings removed as data-entry errors.
  • Low-missingness categoricals (body_type, transmission, source, etc.) imputed with mode — rate <10%, distribution preserved.
  • original_price (44% missing) retained only for EDA; excluded from ML.
  • kms_run capped at 99th percentile to remove extreme outliers.

Leakage Columns Excluded from ML

broker_quote (r=0.96) emi_starts_from (r=1.0) booking_down_pymnt (r=1.0) original_price (44% missing) times_viewed (post-listing) is_hot (post-listing) reserved (post-listing) car_name (high cardinality) variant (943 unique) rto (261 unique) registered_city (redundant) model (redundant)

Selected Findings

  • Automatic cars average Rs. 7.09 Lakhs vs. manual cars at Rs. 4.08 Lakhs — a 74% premium.
  • Median price drops from Rs. 5.83 L (1-year-old) to Rs. 0.73 L (15-year-old), illustrating rapid depreciation.
  • First-owner cars (Rs. 4.03 L median) command significantly more than second-owner (Rs. 3.44 L) or third-owner (Rs. 2.77 L).
  • Diesel cars have higher median prices than petrol, driven by SUV and sedan representation.
  • Chennai has the highest median used-car price (Rs. 4.51 L); Kolkata the lowest (Rs. 3.10 L).
  • "Great"-rated cars (Rs. 4.09 L median) dramatically outprice "overpriced"-rated ones (Rs. 1.63 L).
  • Correlation: year of manufacture (+0.44), kms_run (-0.34), total_owners (-0.22) — all in expected directions.

Feature Engineering

  • car_age = 2021 - yr_mfr  (reference year matches peak listing year)
  • kms_per_year = kms_run / car_age  (normalised mileage; avoids age confounding)

Limitations & Future Work

Limitations

  • 7,400 listings; larger data would improve generalisation
  • Single-platform source; may not represent the full market
  • Listing prices, not final transaction prices
  • Luxury brands under-represented (<1% each)
  • Data from 2019–2021; market has evolved since
  • Only 13 cities; no rural or tier-2/3 coverage

Future Improvements

  • Larger, more recent datasets (2022–2024)
  • Engine displacement, mileage (km/L), safety ratings
  • Hyperparameter tuning via GridSearchCV
  • XGBoost, LightGBM, or CatBoost models
  • Macroeconomic indicators (fuel price, interest rates)
  • Flask / Streamlit web deployment
